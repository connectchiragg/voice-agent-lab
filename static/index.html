Response = end of turn + LLM first token + TTS first audio + a little overhead. Under ~800 ms feels conversational.

How this works
  1. You speak. Your mic streams to the server over WebRTC (or a WebSocket with ?transport=ws).
  2. Deepgram Flux turns speech into text and decides when your turn has ended.
  3. The language model (open-weight, on Groq) writes the reply, sentence by sentence.
  4. Deepgram Aura-2 speaks each sentence as soon as it is written; captions follow the audio.
  5. Barge-in: talk over VALR and it stops within a few hundred milliseconds, remembering only what you heard.
